What's new in 1.9.2 — live allowance, stronger Codex sessions, and worktree polish

• Claude plan allowance stays visible on phone and desktop, with 5-hour and 7-day usage, exact reset times, and a detailed refreshable view.
• Codex sessions are found and resumed more reliably across macOS, Windows and Linux, with native fork, compact and review controls plus clearer failures instead of silent hangs.
• Worktrees now show an explicit creation receipt, can open a session in the new checkout immediately, and keep the main checkout first with newer linked worktrees easy to find.
• Pairing-day reconnects, duplicate resumed history, sheet gestures and bottom-edge controls are more stable and predictable.
