What's new in 2.6.1

- Improved Codex usage details, including available quota reset cards and confirmation when redeeming an eligible card.
- Request an update for a compatible paired computer from Settings, with progress and recovery status shown in the app.
- DeepSeek Harness model choices now retain their provider. If the selected model cannot be applied, your message is kept instead of being sent with another model.
- The copy button now appears after each agent reply, making it easier to copy the answer after reading it.

Update Pairlet on your computer too to use the new capabilities. Quota reset cards are available only when supported by your existing account.
