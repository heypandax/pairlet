What's new in 1.6.1

Added
• Task-context review (preview): hand a colleague a review task instead of your machine — they work in their own repo, with their own AI account and approval policy, and return a structured result. No session control is transferred and nobody enters your computer
• Review Center: draft a request, see what is waiting on you, and send results back from one place. With both apps closed, delivery, retries and history keep running on your computer's daemon
• Review contacts and session-handoff contacts stay separate: a link can only do the one thing it was authorized for

Improved
• Pairing with a colleague now validates their key on the spot, so an invalid QR fails immediately instead of leaving a link that reconnects forever and never works
