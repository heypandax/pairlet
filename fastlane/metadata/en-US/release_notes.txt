What's new in 1.6.0

Added
• Session handoff: a session running on one device can be handed to another and continued there, with context and approval state carried over
• Collaborator links: invite someone into a specific session with a one-time credential — read-only or allowed to drive — and the offer lands directly on their device
• Approvals, upgraded: permission prompts can wait for you indefinitely ("keep waiting for me") instead of auto-denying after 10 minutes, and every approval lands in one reviewable history
• Archivable sessions: tuck away sessions you rarely open; browse and unarchive them all from one place
• Project entry points: pin projects, start a new session right from the recents list, and pick the model when creating one
• Version visibility: check the daemon's version, get update reminders on the phone, one-tap update on desktop/Android
• Feishu groups gain Smart Review (/review), between per-request approval and full trust: an AI screens each request against the group's declared purpose first — clearly low-risk ones run directly (still confined to in-project tools), everything else still asks you. Off by default; enable it on the bridge first, /trust-status to inspect
