What's new in 2.4.0

- Dictation: a slow connection no longer shows a failure first. Uploading and waiting are shown separately, a transcript that arrives late is still used, and retrying does not transcribe the same recording twice.
- Reopening a session you just looked at shows what you already had right away and then adds what is new, so long sessions do not reload their whole history each time.
- Approvals: a card ignores taps for a moment after it appears, so a double tap cannot approve the next request, and its buttons only act on the request they show.
- While a photo is still being compressed the send button waits, instead of sending the message without the photo.
- Settings > About now shows this device's fingerprint and your computer's, so you can check that you paired with your own computer.
- Removed a few rarely used features: review requests, handing a session over to a collaborator, sharing a folder with a guest, and the unencrypted direct-connection option. Normal pairing and sessions are not affected.
- After you also update Pairlet on your computer: Kimi sessions run with the model and permission mode you chose, and a device you revoked on the computer stays revoked after a restart.
