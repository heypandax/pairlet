What's new in 2.1.0

• Collapse finished tool steps in a conversation so the final reply stands out; failed tools, approvals and questions stay visible.
• Managed session list: only sessions you created in Pairlet are listed, and sessions started elsewhere can be imported explicitly.
• Completion, error and quota notifications now reach your phone even while the desktop app is online; repeats within 30 seconds are merged.
• Context usage shows a clear status when no data is available yet, and history sessions from more backends report their usage.
• New sessions start in your default mode, and the mode you picked is kept when switching agents.
• Phone landscape opens a session in a full-width single column; the project list defaults to a flat view; the desktop sidebar folds older projects.
• Project pins sync per computer, iPad multitasking keeps the session state, and image attachments are forwarded to more backends.
