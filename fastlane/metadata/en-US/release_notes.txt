What's new in 1.9.0 — Git, worktrees, rewind, and dsh answers

• Git panel: finish your review on the phone — status, stage, commit, push, pull and branch switching, with two-step confirms for anything destructive.
• Worktrees: see every checkout of a repo, create one for a new or existing branch, and clean them up safely (live sessions are protected).
• Rewind & fork: long-press one of your messages to retry it with different wording, or fork a parallel session from that point. Files are never rolled back — only the conversation.
• dsh sessions: the agent's questions and permission requests now show up on the phone and can be answered there; unanswered requests time out safely instead of hanging the turn.
