What's new in 2.3.0

- The name is now Pairlet everywhere, and the icon no longer carries the Pocket label. It is the same app: pairing and data are kept, and there is nothing to reinstall or pair again.
- Tapping a project now shows "Opening…" and offers a retry if the computer does not answer; projects with many sessions no longer fail to open.
- When Claude asks a question, tap the card's title to collapse it to one line and read what came before; your selections are kept. The "Answer required" notice at the top now takes a single line.
- The Codex model list is no longer limited to three built-in models, and it says why when only the built-in ones can be shown.
- After you also update Pairlet on your computer: Kimi background tasks no longer stay "running" after they end, ZCode 3.14 sessions can send messages again, and DeepSeek sessions started on the phone show up on the computer too.
