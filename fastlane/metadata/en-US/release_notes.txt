What's new in 2.5.0

- Long conversations open faster: recent messages load first, with more history added as needed. Scrolling back no longer duplicates loaded history or new messages.
- Images in history load as previews first. Tap an image to fetch the original, reducing the wait on slower connections.
- More reliable connections: fewer unnecessary reconnects on slow networks. When you return to the same local network, Pairlet switches back to a direct connection while idle, keeping the current connection during transfers and replies.
- A new + button at the top of a conversation lets you start another session in the current project.
- Fixed Follow System always using dark mode. Appearance now follows your system setting.
- More reliable approval and question notifications, even when another device is online.
- Model lists appear from cache and refresh in the background, reducing waits and preventing older lists from replacing newer models.

Update Pairlet on your computer too for the full connection and history improvements.
