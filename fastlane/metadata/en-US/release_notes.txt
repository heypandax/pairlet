What's new in 2.6.1

- Choose what happens after dictation: put the text in the composer, or enable correction before sending. Preview the text and highlighted changes; if correction fails or a change needs review, the text stays available for you to check.
- Improved session recovery when switching between Wi-Fi and cellular networks. Fixed missing session content after returning to a local connection.
- Fixed history loading for newer DeepSeek Harness sessions, preserving messages and tool results.
- Cleaner session lists with fewer internal subtask records, and improved session grouping after a project is moved.

Update Pairlet on your computer too. Voice correction requires a compatible computer app and a configured agent. A disclosure explains data use before you first enable it.
