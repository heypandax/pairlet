Before you start

CC Pocket remote-controls coding agents that run on your own computer. Here is exactly where your data goes.

end-to-end encrypted
phone relay forwards ciphertext your computer
What is sent
Prompts you type, images you attach, and voice you dictate.
Where it goes
End-to-end encrypted to your own paired computer — nowhere else. The relay only forwards ciphertext and cannot read it.
>_
Who processes it
The coding agent CLI you installed and signed in on your computer (for example Claude Code). It talks to its model provider under your own account and terms. CC Pocket includes no third-party AI service and holds no AI credentials.
Voice dictation
Transcribed by Apple's speech service by default, or optionally on your own computer — never by any cloud AI service of ours.
Demo mode runs entirely on this device with sample data.
Privacy policy
Agree and continue
