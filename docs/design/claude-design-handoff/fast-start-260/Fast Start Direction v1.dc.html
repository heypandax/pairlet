The mobile form: FAB into a bottom sheet

Owner decision · the in-place entry row is gone and the list has that height back

Idle Projects is now only Projects: nav, machine state, list. The single accent circle at the bottom right is the whole new-task affordance, and it reads as one thing rather than as a row of chrome the eye has to parse — an icon-only ＋ at 56 dp on the accent, with real elevation and a short scrim gradient beneath it so it stays legible over a scrolling row. Tapping it raises the sheet that was designed in the earlier round, unchanged: multiline field on top, accessory row below with 项目 and 智能体 on the left and 发送 on the right. The two creation-time decisions are still visible before a word is typed; they simply live in the surface that creation happens in, not on the surface that supervision happens on. Dismissal is a scrim tap or a swipe on the grabber, and the draft survives it — the sheet is a place you can leave and come back to, which is what makes covering the list acceptable here where it was not acceptable for a permanent bar.
